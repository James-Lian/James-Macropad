## Case CAD model
The case is made up of two parts; a top section and a bottom section. The PCB is mounted plateless on the bottom section. <img width="946" height="628" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1968c931-d70d-4938-b0b0-71b5a0e8fe90" />

I've also designed a custom knob to fit on the rotary encoder. I've taken inspiration from the knob on the OrpheusPad, while adding some adjustments to ensure that the knob is smaller so it fits better between the rotary encoder and the keycaps.
